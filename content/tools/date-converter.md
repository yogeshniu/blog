### About the dates

This converter uses the same month-length table as the [Nepali Calendar](/tools/nepali-calendar/) and covers **2000 to 2090 BS** (14 April 1943 to 13 April 2034 AD).

Dates up to 2083 BS are verified. Dates from 2084 BS are **estimates** and are labelled as such, because the official calendar for those years is not published yet. For legal or official documents, always confirm the date against the official calendar.
