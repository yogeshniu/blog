Type a title exactly as you want it to appear: capitalisation is not changed. Authors go one per line as "Last, First Middle". Nothing you type leaves your browser.

Harvard follows the Cite Them Right layout, and the IEEE output follows the usual IEEE reference style. Universities and journals add their own rules, so compare with your style guide.
