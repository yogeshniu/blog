Mark a card "Got it" and it comes back after 1, 3, 7, 14 and then 30 days. Mark it "Again" and it returns in the same session. Your deck and progress are saved only in this browser, so clearing site data removes them.

Paste cards from a spreadsheet or Anki export: one card per line, front and back separated by a tab or a | character.
