### About the dates

- Covers **2000 to 2090 BS** (14 April 1943 to 13 April 2034). "Today" uses Nepal time.
- **Verified through 2083 BS.** The month lengths were cross-checked between five open-source Nepali date libraries, which agree on every month, and sample dates were checked against published calendars (Nepali New Year each year, Constitution Day, Republic Day, and a full month-by-month table for 2083).
- One month differs between those libraries: the start of Jestha 2062 (14 or 15 May 2005). Published calendars show 14 May, which is what this tool uses.
- **2084 to 2090 BS are estimates** and are shown with a striped pattern. The official calendar for those years is not published yet, and the sources disagree by a day in some months.
- Saturday is shown in red because it is the weekly holiday in Nepal. Festivals and public holidays are not included.
- Keyboard: arrow keys move the selected day, Page Up and Page Down change month.
